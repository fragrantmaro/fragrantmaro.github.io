DuckyAI Player

Offline AI Subtitle Player

Play your videos and get AI-generated subtitles right on your phone. No internet connection needed.

What it does

  • Completely offline

    Subtitles are generated on your device, so your videos stay on your phone.

  • Subtitles as you watch

    Open a video without subtitles and get near-real-time AI subtitles during playback.

  • Your model, your language

    Pick Performance for speed or Quality for accuracy, and set a default subtitle language.

  • Export to SRT

    Regenerate, rename, delete, or export subtitles for use in your editing tools.
